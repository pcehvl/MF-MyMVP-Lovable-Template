## Evidence

- "I post in three different Facebook groups and still don't know if anyone
  has the book."
- "Last semester I paid full price because I gave up looking."
- Observed: she searched by course code, not by book title.

## Interpretation

- Finding a specific used book is slow and uncertain; the effort makes
  students give up and buy new. *(interpretation)*
- Course code may be the natural search key for students. *(interpretation,
  needs more interviews)*
